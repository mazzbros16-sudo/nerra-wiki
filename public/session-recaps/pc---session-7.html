PC - Session 7

PC - Session 7

Oct 16, 20268 min read

For the first time since arriving in Needleberry, the party found themselves beyond the borders of the settlement. The road north stretched ahead through the winter wilderness, partially buried beneath fresh snow. Forests crowded either side of the path, their branches heavy with white, while the distant peaks of the Stonewood Spine remained somewhere beyond the horizon.

For Dean, this was the furthest he had ever travelled from home. Although he knew the surrounding countryside through the stories of travellers who had passed through the Bent Needle, the reality proved somewhat less impressive. Famous trees were smaller than expected. Great rocks were merely rocks. Nevertheless, Dean remained determined to appreciate each landmark, no matter how disappointing it might be in person.

As daylight faded, the party brought their cart to a stop and established their first camp. Ash began gathering herbs, moss and other useful materials from the nearby wilderness, while Flint collected enough firewood to see them through the evening. Dean prepared a substantial stew, momentarily forgetting that neither Flint nor Hemlock required food and leaving Ash as the only person capable of helping him finish it.

With camp established and the horses secured as competently as the party could manage, Flynn entered his standby state before taking watch over the others.

Some time later, he heard movement amongst the trees. At first, it appeared to be nothing more than an animal passing through the undergrowth. Then Flint saw a larger silhouette moving along the road. He woke Dean.

Looking into the darkness, Dean saw a heavily robed figure approaching the camp. The stranger was completely covered in mismatched layers of cloth, with only a pair of faded blue eyes visible beneath the wrappings. A large shield rested across his back, a sword hung at his side and the sound of metal beneath the robes suggested that he was wearing armour.

Ash slipped quietly from the cart and circled into the trees while Dean confronted the stranger directly. The figure claimed to be searching for a companion. His voice sounded familiar.

After several tense questions, it became increasingly clear that the stranger was searching for Vadim. The party told him what had happened at Belcons Manor and explained that Vadim had been cremated according to his final wishes. When shown Vadim’s locket, the stranger opened it and stared at the two figures within. He then introduced himself. His name was Aleksander. Vadim had been his son.

The party explained how Vadim had fought beside them, protected the people of Needleberry and ultimately sacrificed himself during the battle at Belcons Manor. Aleksander listened quietly. Though he had arrived too late to find his son alive, the party assured him that Vadim had not died alone. He had been surrounded by people who valued him, even if he had spent much of their short time together stubbornly refusing medical attention.

Before the conversation could continue, a scream echoed from somewhere within the forest. The party immediately followed the sound. They emerged into a clearing containing a small camp belonging to refugees from the nearby settlement of Canterbury. Several frightened survivors stood huddled together while a familiar pale creature fed upon a body at their feet.

It was another vampire thrall. Unlike those encountered beneath Needleberry, this creature appeared well-fed. Its body was less withered, its movements stronger and its attention immediately turned towards the newcomers.

Ash charged forward and struck it with an alchemical mixture of fire and vitality. The attack burned into the creature, but also drew its attention away from the refugees. It retaliated with terrifying speed. A single blow knocked Ash unconscious.

Dean rushed forward and used his magic to protect and stabilise Ash while Flint attempted to exploit the creature’s supernatural vulnerabilities. Aleksander advanced behind them, shield raised, and seized the thrall before it could reach the remaining refugees.

As Aleksander grappled with the creature, he pulled the cloth away from his mouth. Fangs extended from beneath his lips. He attempted to bite the thrall. Despite the sudden revelation, the party continued their attack. Ash regained consciousness and hurled another alchemical concoction, weakening the creature against acid, fire and slashing weapons. Dean’s magic protected Aleksander as he held it in place, allowing Flint to close the distance.

With the creature trapped in Alexander’s grasp, Flint struck. His blade tore through its throat, severing its head and sending it rolling from Aleksander’s arms. The creature collapsed and began to burn away into ash.

Three of the Canterbury refugees remained alive. They explained that creatures like this one had attacked their settlement several nights earlier and killed most of its population. The survivors had fled south, seeking whatever safety they could find.

The party helped them gather their belongings and warned them that the bodies of the dead would need to be burned before they could rise again. Dean offered the survivors what remained of the evening’s stew and told them to travel towards Needleberry. The Bent Needle had been left in the care of the community and would provide them with shelter.

The survivors accepted, though their gratitude became noticeably more cautious once they realised the party was now travelling with a vampire. The following morning, the two groups separated. The refugees began their journey towards Needleberry while the party continued north.

During the journey, Aleksander finally explained what he was. Before becoming a vampire, he and his wife Nadine had worked as monster hunters. They guarded travellers, protected settlements and removed dangerous creatures from places where ordinary people could not defend themselves.

Their final contract led them to a mansion in the north. The person who had hired them was not its victim, but its master. Malcorvus. Alexander and Elara were captured. Alexander was drained and transformed, while Elara made an agreement to protect their son. She would serve Malcorvus if Vadim was allowed to live.

Malcovbus kept his word only in the cruellest possible manner. Vadim survived, but was forced to witness what his parents had become. Elara remained under Malcorvus’ control, while Aleksander was imprisoned and tortured until he eventually escaped. By the time Aleksander returned, the mansion had been abandoned. Elara, Vadim and Malcorvus were gone. Alexander had been searching for them ever since.

Unlike the thralls the party had encountered, Aleksander retained his independence. He claimed that greater vampires could exert control over those beneath them, but he had somehow broken free of Malcorvus’ influence. He still required blood, though he insisted that he did not kill those he fed upon.

He also shared what he knew of vampire weaknesses. Sunlight was dangerous to them, as were fire and forms of vitality magic intended to harm the undead. Older vampires were generally more powerful, and many lesser vampires remained bound to the will of those who created them. With Vadim dead and vampires apparently responsible for it, Aleksander’s path now led in the same direction as the party’s. North.

The discussion eventually turned towards Flint and his strange connection to the vampire creatures. When Dwarven commands were spoken around him, Flint reacted automatically, declaring that his obedience protocols had been disengaged. The response unsettled Alexander, who recognised something in it resembling the supernatural domination used by powerful vampires.

Ash offered to investigate Flynn’s internal mechanisms. The cart was brought to a stop and Flint allowed Ash to open one of the panels along his back. Ash had barely begun examining the unfamiliar machinery when a violent surge of electricity erupted from inside Flint’s body.

Ash was thrown across the road and into a nearby tree. The shock was powerful enough to knock him unconscious and warp the screwdriver still clenched in his hand. Dean used his magic to restore him, although Ash remained dazed and barely responsive for some time afterwards.

As the party resumed their journey, Flint suddenly stood rigidly upright. His eyes turned red. He began speaking in harsh, guttural Dwarven. Then, just as quickly, his eyes returned to normal and his body relaxed. Flint appeared to have no memory of what had happened.

Whatever existed within him was not merely damaged machinery. Something remained connected to his past, and potentially to whatever awaited them in the north. The party continued along the road towards the small settlement of Waymeet. Beyond it lay Ironwall, the Stonewood Spine and the mysteries drawing each of them forward. Four days of wilderness travel still remained.
