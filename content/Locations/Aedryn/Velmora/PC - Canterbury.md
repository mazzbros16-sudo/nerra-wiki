Canterbury is a small settlement located somewhere north of [[PC - Needleberry]].

The party encountered several refugees who claimed that Canterbury had been attacked by creatures similar to those beneath Needleberry. According to the survivors, most of the settlement's population was killed.

The remaining refugees fled south. After rescuing them from a vampire thrall, the party directed them towards Needleberry and the shelter available within [[PC - The Bent Needle]].

The present condition of Canterbury is unknown.
