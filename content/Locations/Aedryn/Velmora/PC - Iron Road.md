The Iron Road is an enormous dwarven-built transport route running through [[PC - Velmora]] towards [[PC - Ironwall]] and the [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].

The party first reached the Iron Road while travelling north from [[PC - Needleberry]] with [[PC - Tobias and Edwin Marrick]]. Compared with the remote road they had been following, the change was immediate. The Iron Road is formed from enormous fitted stone slabs and is wide enough for several wagons to travel beside one another. Railway tracks run through its centre, while carts, wagons and riders use the surrounding lanes.

Much of the road remains warm even during winter. Snow melts almost immediately from its surface and runs into drainage channels along its edges, allowing trade to continue despite the surrounding countryside being covered in snow. The party encountered at least one section where this system had stopped functioning, although they were unable to determine how the heating system itself works.

Steam locomotives travel along the central railway, carrying passengers and large quantities of freight between settlements along the route. Although the railway was originally dwarven technology, the trains currently operating upon it were rebuilt after the [[PC - Smoke War]] and are now used primarily for transport and trade.

The route remains heavily travelled throughout winter and contains rest areas, supply stops and other facilities designed to keep travellers and goods moving.

## Wartime Legacy

The Iron Road predates the Smoke War and once connected the dwarven territories of the Stoneward Spine with the lands beyond them. During the war it became an important military and supply route.

Evidence of that history remains visible along the road.

During their journey, the party discovered the stripped remains of a dwarven war machine preserved beside the highway as a memorial. Locals still stop there to remember those killed by the machine during the war.

Although its internal machinery has been removed, the shell remains where it fell.

For [[PC - Ash]], it provided one particularly important piece of information:

The machines could be destroyed.
