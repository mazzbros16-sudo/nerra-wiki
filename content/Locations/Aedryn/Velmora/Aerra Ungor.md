Aerra Ungor was a dwarven-controlled settlement during the Smoke War, known as **Matiel** before its colonisation. The settlement was the site of a lengthy siege led by [[PC - Irilard Belcons]], lasting approximately a year and a half before eventually falling. Following the siege, Belcons took one of Aerra Ungor's large dwarven-reinforced defensive walls as recompense, later incorporating it into the defences of [[PC - Belcons Manor]].
