
The home of [[PC - Irilard Belcons]], situated above [[PC - Needleberry]]. The manor is considerably grander than most buildings surrounding the town and contains numerous artefacts and pieces of military equipment collected by Belcons following the Smoke War.

The manor possesses extensive defensive measures, including traps, reinforced sections, a secure vault and a large dwarven defensive wall taken from [[PC - Aerra Ungor]]. Many of these systems rely upon a dwarven generator located towards the older portion of the manor.

During the attack on the manor, the generator was mysteriously shut down without being damaged. The party successfully restarted it while fighting three vampiric creatures before retreating back behind the manor's inner defences.
