
Belcons Manor sits atop the only hill in Needleberry, it's sheer cliff face overlooking the town.

Belcons Manor stands two storeys high. Built almost entirely from pale stone, the building does not appear to be as old as much of Needleberry. It's stone does not seem very worn, outside of a small section towards the back.

Belcons manor is only about 15 years old. Previously, the mayor lived in the old manor that is now the back section of Belcons manor. But Belcons took possession and included it in his new design when he moved to the town. 

Even beneath several days of snowfall, landscaped gardens remain visible around the property. Small hedges line the path towards the entrance, it's grand entrace.
