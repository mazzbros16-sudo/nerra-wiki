Tinker's Row is the repair and craft district of [[PC - Waymeet]], running alongside the main flow of traffic through the town.

Damaged wagons are constantly being brought into the Row from [[PC - Iron Road]]. Blacksmiths, wheelwrights, wainwrights, farriers and other craftspeople operate from workshops lining the street, repairing everything from broken wheels and damaged axles to harnesses and travelling equipment.

The district operates with remarkable speed. Wagons are inspected, moved into workshops, repaired and returned to the road as quickly as possible. The constant flow of travellers means work continues well into the night.

The party first entered Tinker's Row after helping a traveller named George move his wagon from the main road after one of its wheels split.

Unfortunately, an inspection of their own wagon revealed a considerably larger problem.

The rough road from [[PC - Needleberry]] had badly damaged its axle. The wagon would not survive the journey to [[PC - Ironwall]] without extensive repairs, leaving the party stranded in [[PC - Waymeet]] for at least a day.

[[PC - Ash]] chose to remain in Tinker's Row and work through the night as an apprentice, helping repair wagons while learning more about their construction.

By the following morning, an entirely different problem had emerged.

The authorities had begun inspecting wagons throughout Waymeet following the theft of a valuable shipment.

Including proof of ownership.

Which the party's wagon does not possess.
