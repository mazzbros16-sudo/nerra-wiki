The Wayfarer's Rest is a busy travellers' inn in [[PC - Waymeet]].

It caters primarily to ordinary travellers passing along [[PC - Iron Road]], including teamsters, mercenaries, hunters and adventurers. The inn is crowded, loud and considerably less polished than some of Waymeet's wealthier establishments.

Its common room is filled with travellers exchanging news, stories and rumours from across [[PC - Aedryn]]. The walls carry names, markings and messages left behind by previous visitors, while noticeboards advertise work and departing caravans.

The party arrived at the Wayfarer's Rest during their first night in Waymeet.

[[PC - Dean]] quickly discovered that the establishment was understaffed and offered his services behind the bar in exchange for food and accommodation. He spent much of the night serving drinks, speaking with travellers and attempting to gather information about the town and the road ahead.

[[PC - Flint]] later spoke with [[PC - Rogar]], an orc from [[PC - The Shattercoast]], about the route to [[PC - Ironwall]], the Stoneward Spine and the lingering consequences of the [[PC - Smoke War]].

The following morning, the Wayfarer's Rest became considerably more important when the party learned that someone associated with the inn may be capable of acquiring wagon papers without troubling the authorities about where the wagon came from.
