Ash arrived in [[PC - Needleberry]] shortly before the first attack upon [[PC - The Bent Needle]]. His presence as a Blightwarden initially drew uneasy attention from the townsfolk, although one child approached him without fear.

After one of the creatures attacked the tavern, Ash joined Dean, Flint and [[PC - Vadim]] in pursuing it through the town. His alchemist's fire proved decisive in the resulting battle, helping destroy the creature.

Examining what remained revealed something deeply concerning to Ash. The influence of [[PC - The Rift]] appeared to linger around the creature.

While sheltering inside Bromm Hartwell's smithy during the blizzard, Ash warned the others about the dangers of occult practices and spoke about Malach Vey. He later helped investigate the attacks, gathering supplies and following the creatures' trail into [[PC - The Cave]].

Inside the cave, Ash helped the party discover the creatures' vulnerability to sunlight. Mirrors found within the upper chamber allowed shafts of light to be directed against them, weakening and blinding the creatures long enough for the party to escape.

After returning to Needleberry, Ash spoke privately with Irilard Belcons about his plan to protect the town. Although Belcons was acting upon an assumption, Ash understood that bringing the party to the Town Hall could place everyone gathered there in danger if the creatures were deliberately pursuing them.

Ash prepared part of the defence around [[PC - Belcons Manor]], creating a line of fire across the approach that could be remotely ignited. When the creatures arrived, he activated the trap and set several of them alight before the manor was sealed.

During the battle in the generator room, Ash helped restart the dwarven machinery by opening the fuel valves and timing the final release. He also used alchemist's fire against the creatures and repeatedly provided medical assistance to fallen members of the party.

Following Flint's sudden malfunction, Ash examined the automaton and determined that his systems remained functional. The problem appeared to originate within Flint's mind rather than from conventional physical damage.

When the red-eyed creature broke through the dining hall's defences, Ash ignited the mustard gas released by the manor's traps. The resulting fire destroyed many of the lesser creatures, though the red-eyed creature continued advancing.

After Vadim was killed and Dean was struck down, Ash noticed that the creature appeared reluctant to attack Flint. He pulled Dean behind the automaton, using Flint as protection until the creature recognised his Custodian authority and withdrew.

In the aftermath, Ash repaired damaged sections of Belcons Manor, secured the area and confirmed that the settlement's defences had re-engaged.

Before departing Needleberry, Ash completed Hemlock, a clockwork raven familiar designed to assist him with alchemical work and combat.

Bromm later advised both Ash and Flint to seek out the Redenten within [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]], believing their technological knowledge might prove useful.

Ash left Needleberry with Dean and Flint, accompanying them towards the Stonewood Spine.

During the party's first night on the road, Ash helped defend several refugees from a vampire thrall. His initial attack drew the creature away from the survivors, but its retaliation knocked him unconscious. After recovering, Ash used another alchemical mixture to weaken the thrall against acid, fire and slashing weapons.

Ash later attempted to investigate Flint's internal mechanisms. When he opened one of the automaton's rear panels, a powerful electrical discharge threw him into a nearby tree and knocked him unconscious. The surge also warped the screwdriver he had been using.

Although Dean restored him, Ash remained severely dazed for some time afterwards. His examination did not reveal the source of Flint's unusual behaviour.
