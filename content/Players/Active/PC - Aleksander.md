Aleksander is a heavily armoured traveller encountered by the party during their first night beyond [[PC - Needleberry]]. He was searching for [[PC - Vadim]], whom he identified as his son after recognising the locket carried by the party.

Alexander was once a monster hunter who travelled alongside his wife, [[PC - Elara]]. According to his account, they were captured after accepting a contract that led them into a trap arranged by [[PC - Malcorvus von Helvig]]

Alexander was transformed into a vampire and held under Malcorvus' control. He eventually escaped and now appears capable of resisting the supernatural influence exerted by greater vampires. He still requires blood, but claims that he does not kill those upon whom he feeds.

After learning of Vadim's death, Alexander joined the party on their journey north. He is searching for Elara and Malcorvus and believes that both may still be alive.

Alexander carries a large shield and sword beneath layers of mismatched robes. During a fight with a vampire thrall, he demonstrated considerable physical strength by holding the creature in place while the others attacked it.
