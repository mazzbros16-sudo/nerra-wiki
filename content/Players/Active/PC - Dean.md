Dean Picklepots was the owner and keeper of [[PC - The Bent Needle]], one of the central gathering places within [[PC - Needleberry]].

When the creatures first attacked the town, Dean joined Flint, Ash and [[PC - Vadim]] in pursuing one of them through the streets. The trail led to The Pincushion, where they found Edda Voss and her husband under attack. Although the party killed the creature, Edda was later found dying beneath the snow outside her shop. She passed away in Dean's arms.

Dean remained closely involved in the defence of Needleberry. He helped investigate the creatures, gathered supplies from The Pincushion and followed their trail to [[PC - The Cave]]. Inside, he discovered a boarded passage that allowed the party to escape the pursuing creatures and make use of sunlight against them.

After returning to town, Dean supported Irilard Belcons' plan to draw the creatures towards [[PC - Belcons Manor]], though only on the condition that Needleberry's children were first brought somewhere safe. He later addressed the gathered townsfolk, explaining that the party intended to draw the creatures away from the settlement.

During the defence of Belcons Manor, Dean repeatedly used the building and its machinery against the creatures. Within the generator room, he released coal onto several attackers, doused another in fuel and helped restart the generator by operating its pressure pump.

Dean witnessed Belcons attempt to exchange the party's lives for the safety of Needleberry. He also noticed Belcons activate an unknown mechanism immediately before the red-eyed creature killed him.

In the battle that followed, Dean continued fighting while Flint became unresponsive. He was eventually struck down by the red-eyed creature, but survived after Ash pulled him behind Flint. The creature hesitated to attack the automaton and eventually recognised Flint's authority before leaving.

Following the battle, Dean helped dispose of the creatures and performed a symbolic sacrifice for those killed during the attacks. While searching Belcons Manor, he also uncovered information concerning [[PC - The Smoke War]], the Liberation of Vellor and the Night of Red Smoke at Karnholt.

Once the remaining threat appeared to have passed, Dean escorted the rescued children back into town. After learning that the Town Hall had been attacked, he helped organise a mob within The Bent Needle and returned to the cave in search of the escaped creature.

Before leaving Needleberry, Dean transferred ownership of [[PC - The Bent Needle]] to Bromm Hartwell, asking that the building be used for the benefit of the community. He held a final feast for the survivors and publicly announced his departure.

Dean also reached an understanding with Mayor Hammond, who rescinded his outstanding taxation notice. Dean nevertheless warned the mayor to treat the people of Needleberry fairly after he was gone.

Dean ultimately departed Needleberry alongside Ash and Flint. His search for Lagertha now takes him towards the [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]], guided in part by the riddle she left behind:

**"The unrestrained pursuit of knowledge knows what is unknowable."**

The journey marked the first time Dean had travelled any significant distance beyond Needleberry.

During the party's first night on the road, Dean met [[PC - Aleksander]], who revealed himself to be Vadim's father. Dean helped explain how Vadim had died and assured Alexander that his son had not been alone during his final days.

When the party encountered refugees from [[PC - Canterbury]] under attack, Dean used his magic to protect and revive Ash. After the fight, he fed the survivors and directed them towards Needleberry, where the Bent Needle could provide them with shelter.

Dean later used his magic to revive Ash after a violent electrical discharge from Flint knocked him unconscious. He continues travelling north alongside Ash, Flint and Alexander.
