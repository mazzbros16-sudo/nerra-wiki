Vadim encountered Flint wandering the frozen roads of Southern Aedryn. Recognising the signs of an approaching blizzard, he led the lost automaton towards the nearby settlement of [[PC - Needleberry]].

Shortly after their arrival, the creatures attacked [[PC - The Bent Needle]]. Vadim joined Flint, Dean and Ash in pursuing one of them through the settlement and helped defeat it outside The Pincushion.

Vadim assisted with the investigation into the attacks. When the trail from the abandoned Needleworks disappeared beneath the snow, the party continued searching until they reached [[PC - The Cave]] beyond Needleberry.

Within the cave, the party discovered numerous creatures standing motionless within an abandoned camp. After an unseen voice awakened them, Vadim narrowly escaped being overwhelmed and reached a smaller chamber where the party used sunlight to fight their pursuers.

After returning to Needleberry, Vadim openly opposed Irilard Belcons' proposal that the party remain within [[PC - Belcons Manor]]. He accused Belcons of hiding while the townsfolk prepared to defend themselves and declared that he would rather die protecting Needleberry than survive behind the manor's walls.

Vadim eventually agreed that the party should remain together, but only on the condition that he stay outside the manor and ensure that the creatures came for them rather than the town.

That evening, Vadim positioned himself outside as bait. When the red-eyed creature appeared among the trees, Vadim acknowledged it and ran for the manor, drawing the approaching creatures into Ash's prepared line of fire.

Within the generator room, Vadim was one of the first party members brought down by the attacking creatures. After recovering, he killed the final creature before it could overwhelm Dean. Vadim then physically held a damaged pressure pipe closed while the party restarted the generator.

After Belcons' death and the collapse of the dining hall's defences, Vadim continued holding the line against the creatures while Flint remained unresponsive. When the party escaped through the manor window, the red-eyed creature pursued them with extraordinary speed. Vadim stopped alongside the others to face it.

The creature struck him with overwhelming force. Vadim was impaled and thrown aside. He showed no signs of life and did not recover.

After the battle, Vadim's body was cremated by his companions.

Vadim died defending the people of Needleberry, having refused to abandon the town or allow others to face the creatures alone.

After leaving Needleberry, the party encountered [[PC - Aleksander]], who identified himself as Vadim's father after recognising Vadim's locket.

According to Alexander, Vadim was the son of two monster hunters. His parents accepted a contract that led them into a trap arranged by [[PC - Malcorvus von Helvig]]. Alexander was transformed into a vampire, while Vadim's mother, [[PC - Elara]], agreed to serve Malcorvus in exchange for her son's life.

Alexander believes that Vadim witnessed what happened to his parents before the family was separated. He had been searching for Vadim ever since escaping Malcorvus' control.

The party told Alexander that Vadim had fought to protect Needleberry and that he had died surrounded by people who cared about him.
