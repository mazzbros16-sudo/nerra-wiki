The Mountain Sentinels are the branch of the [[PC - Crown Guard of Velmor]] responsible for the territories surrounding [[PC - Ironwall]] and the [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].

The party first learned more about them after arriving in [[PC - Waymeet]].

The Sentinels have a reputation for being considerably stricter than other branches of the Crown Guard. Their responsibilities include policing the region around Ironwall, protecting the mountain routes and dealing with crime and contraband moving towards or away from the Stoneward Spine.

Waymeet itself maintains only a limited local law-enforcement presence. More serious offenders may instead be detained and sent to Ironwall for the Mountain Sentinels to investigate.

This became immediately relevant to the party when a valuable shipment was stolen in Waymeet. Authorities responded by inspecting wagons throughout the town, including their cargo and ownership documentation.

According to the craftsmen of [[PC - Tinker's Row]], anyone unable to satisfactorily explain their wagon or cargo may find themselves sent to Ironwall.

The party currently possesses a wagon without ownership papers.
