Dwarven war machines were enormous mechanical weapons used by the dwarves during the [[PC - Smoke War]].

The party encountered the remains of one beside [[PC - Iron Road]] while travelling towards [[PC - Waymeet]].

The machine had been destroyed during the war and its internal mechanisms had long since been stripped away, leaving only its enormous armoured shell. Rather than removing it, the wreck had been left beside the road as a memorial to those it killed.

Locals and travellers still stop at the site to pay their respects.

To [[PC - Ash]], the wreck was particularly significant. Much of his knowledge of dwarven machinery came from stories of the Smoke War and the destruction such machines were capable of causing.

Seeing one destroyed provided a considerably different lesson.

Whatever these machines once were, they were not invincible.
