
The party was tasked by [[PC - Irilard Belcons]] with investigating the creatures responsible for the attacks upon [[PC - Needleberry]] after proving themselves capable of killing one during the first night.

Their investigation led from the abandoned Needleworks to [[PC - The Cave]] outside town, where they discovered a large group of [[PC - Needleberry Creatures]] gathered within what appeared to be an abandoned dwarven camp.

The attacks eventually followed the party to [[PC - Belcons Manor]]. With the children of Needleberry sheltered inside the manor, the party attempted to draw the creatures away from the rest of the town.

The resulting siege ended with the death of [[PC - Irilard Belcons]] and [[PC - Vadim]], but the party survived. During the battle, [[PC - Flint]] discovered that the creatures recognised him as a **Custodian**. After recognising Flint's authority, the [[PC - Red-Eyed Creature]] abandoned the battle, declaring that it was **"Returning Home."**

The remaining creatures within the manor were dealt with and Needleberry survived the attacks.

The following day, however, another creature attacked the Town Hall, killing two townsfolk and abducting another. The party organised the townsfolk and returned to [[PC - The Cave]], where they destroyed another lesser creature but found no sign of the Red-Eyed Creature.

Flint was able to sense that it had travelled north towards [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].

With the immediate threat to Needleberry ended, the party left the settlement behind.

Their pursuit of the escaped creature continues in [[Track the Creature Home]].
