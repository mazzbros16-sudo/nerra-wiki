**Status:** Active

During the battle at [[PC - Belcons Manor]], [[PC - Flint]] discovered that the [[PC - Needleberry Creatures]] recognised him as a **Custodian**.

When confronted by Flint, the larger [[PC - Red-Eyed Creature]] recognised his authority but declared that there was a fault in its mission parameters.

**"Custodian authority recognised. Fault in mission parameters. Returning Home."**

The creature then abandoned the battle and disappeared.

After the party returned to [[PC - The Cave]], Flint concentrated upon the strange connection he had previously felt towards the creatures. He was able to determine that the Red-Eyed Creature had travelled far to the north, towards [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].

The creature's destination remains unknown, as does the meaning of **Home**.

The party has left [[PC - Needleberry]] and begun travelling north in pursuit of the creature.

## Objectives

- Follow the Red-Eyed Creature towards [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].
- Discover where the creature means by **Home**.
- Determine why the creatures recognise [[PC - Flint]] as a Custodian.
- Learn what connection Flint has to the creatures and their origins.

## Current Progress

The party is travelling north along the road towards [[PC - Waymeet]] and [[PC - Ironwall]].

The Red-Eyed Creature remains somewhere ahead of them.
