Elara is the wife of [[PC - Alexander]] and mother of [[PC - Vadim]].

According to Alexander, the two once travelled together as monster hunters, protecting travellers and settlements from dangerous creatures.

Their final contract led them into a trap arranged by [[PC - Malcorvus von Helvig]]. To protect Vadim, Elara agreed to serve Malcorvus in exchange for her son's life. She was subsequently transformed and placed under the vampire's control.

Alexander believes that Elara may still be alive. Her present location and condition are unknown.
