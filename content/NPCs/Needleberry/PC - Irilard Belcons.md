
Irilard Belcons is the owner of the [[PC - Needleberry]] and much of the surrounding township of Needleberry. A former captain of the Velmoran Alliance, Belcons fought extensively during the Smoke War before eventually settling in Needleberry, where he has become one of the town's wealthiest and most influential figures. His home, [[PC - Belcons Manor]], sits above the township and contains an extensive collection of weapons, armour and other items acquired during the war.

Belcons led the year-and-a-half siege of [[PC - Aerra Ungor]], a dwarven-controlled settlement formerly known as Matiel. Following its fall, he took one of the settlement's dwarven-reinforced defensive walls as recompense and later incorporated it into his manor. His experiences during the war continue to shape the way he approaches danger, favouring preparation, strong defences and calculated decisions when the safety of Needleberry is threatened.

Following the attack on [[PC - Needleberry]], Irilard Belcons tasked the party with investigating the creatures responsible. When they returned from discovering the creatures' camp, Belcons encouraged them to remain at [[PC - Belcons Manor]], believing the creatures had demonstrated enough restraint that their attacks might have some greater purpose.

When the creatures eventually attacked the manor, Belcons helped organise its defence and ensured the children brought from Needleberry were placed within his safe room. After the generator was disabled, he accompanied the party through the manor to restart it.

Once the generator was restored, Belcons disappeared into the vault and attempted to negotiate with the creatures under Kharad Vorn He admitted that he had suspected the creatures might follow the party and that their presence at the manor had allowed him to test this theory. Believing Needleberry had no hope of defeating them directly, he offered the lives of the party in exchange for the rest of the town, or at minimum its children.

The [[PC - Red-Eyed Creature]] accepted the possibility of an exchange and required Belcons to meet it face-to-face. Belcons complied, leaving the protection of the vault and approaching the creature personally.

The creature killed him.

Immediately afterwards, it charged towards the party. Before it could reach them, one of the manor's defensive walls slammed shut between them. Dean noticed that immediately before his death, Belcons appeared to press something at his hip.
