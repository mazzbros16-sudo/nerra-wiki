Rogar is an orc originally from [[PC - The Shattercoast]] who currently works at [[PC - Wayfarer's Rest]] in [[PC - Waymeet]].

He has lived in Waymeet for several years and is familiar with the travellers, trade and rumours passing through the town.

[[PC - Flint]] met Rogar during the party's first night in Waymeet. The two spoke about their respective peoples and their shared history beneath dwarven rule.

Rogar described the modern world as one in which both orcs and automatons are still trying to determine their place after the [[PC - Smoke War]]. While some travellers claim life was safer when the dwarves controlled the roads, Rogar has little patience for the argument. Whatever security dwarven rule offered others, his people experienced it in shackles.

Rogar also warned Flint about the journey ahead. The [[PC - Iron Road]] remains relatively safe as far as [[PC - Ironwall]], but the old route into [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]] was devastated during the war. What remains beyond Ironwall is considerably more dangerous.

When Flint asked about less legitimate ways of acquiring equipment in Waymeet, Rogar suggested that it would be unwise for a traveller simply passing through town to go looking for the wrong people.

He did, however, acknowledge that such people exist.
