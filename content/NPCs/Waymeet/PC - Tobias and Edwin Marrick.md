Tobias and Edwin Marrick are brothers and professional cartographers whom the party encountered during the journey north from [[PC - Needleberry]] towards [[PC - Waymeet]].

The brothers were travelling through the region mapping roads and routes when they joined the party's journey.

Despite their profession, the party later discovered that the Marricks had taken a wrong turn after leaving Karnholt, turning what should have been a much shorter journey into several additional weeks on the road.

Neither brother appeared particularly enthusiastic about dwelling on this fact.

The Marricks continued travelling with the party until they reached [[PC - The Iron Road]] and eventually [[PC - Waymeet]].

## Known For

- Professional cartographers.
- Travelling and mapping roads through the region.
- Accompanying the party on their journey to [[PC - Waymeet]].
- Being considerably less reliable at navigation than their profession might suggest.
