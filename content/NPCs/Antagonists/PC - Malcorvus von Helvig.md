Malcorvus von Helvig is a powerful vampire connected to the destruction of [[PC - Vadim]]'s family.

According to [[PC - Aleksander]], Malcorvus arranged a contract that lured Alexander and [[PC - Elara]] into a trap. Alexander was transformed into a vampire, while Elara agreed to serve Malcorvus in exchange for the life of their son.

Alexander claims that Malcorvus is capable of exerting supernatural control over other vampires. He was forced to serve under this influence before somehow breaking free.

By the time Alexander escaped and returned, Malcorvus, Elara and Vadim were gone. Alexander has been searching for them ever since.

Malcorvus' current location is unknown.
