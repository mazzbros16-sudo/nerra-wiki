Pale, elongated humanoid creatures responsible for the attacks upon [[PC - Needleberry]]. Although initially appearing animalistic, subsequent encounters have demonstrated that their behaviour is considerably more organised than first believed.

The creatures appear capable of coordinated movement, following commands and selecting particular targets. They do not simply kill everyone available to them, despite having demonstrated the numbers and strength necessary to cause significantly greater casualties.

A larger [[PC - Red-Eyed Creature]] appears to command them.

During the siege of [[PC - Belcons Manor]], the party discovered that the creatures were capable of infiltrating the manor and somehow shutting down its dwarven generator without visibly damaging it. The Red-Eyed Creature later demonstrated limited speech and an understanding of revenge, identifying those who entered the creatures' camp as enemies whose deaths were a priority.
