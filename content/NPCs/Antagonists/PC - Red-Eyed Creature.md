Red eyes in the storm

> "...Persistence... unexpected..."

> "...Will... still... persists..."

> "...Incomplete..."

> "...Custodian..."

A significantly larger and more intelligent creature that appears to command the vampiric creatures attacking [[PC - Needleberry]]. It was first seen only at a distance during the storm, its red eyes visible from a rooftop as the lesser creatures appeared to form up around it.

The party finally saw the creature clearly during the siege of [[PC - Belcons Manor]]. It is considerably larger than the others, with a hulking body of exposed muscle and sinew, powerful arms, animal-like legs and a sharp, skeletal face. It is capable of speaking broken Common and appears to understand concepts including revenge, enemies and exchange. Most unusually, it recognised  Kharad Vorn and understood its rules surrounding negotiation.

The creature identified those who had entered the creatures' camp as enemies and described their deaths as a priority. After agreeing to an exchange with [[PC - Irilard Belcons]], it demanded that Belcons meet it face-to-face. It then killed him almost effortlessly before attempting to reach the party.

uring the final battle at [[PC - Belcons Manor]], [[PC - Flint]] recovered fragmented memories identifying himself as a **Custodian**. When Flint issued commands in Dwarven, the lesser creatures immediately stopped, although the Red-Eyed Creature eventually resisted his commands.

After killing [[PC - Vadim]] and severely injuring Dean, the creature hesitated when confronted directly by Flint.

It then declared:

**"Custodian authority recognised. Fault in mission parameters. Returning Home."**

The creature abandoned the battle and disappeared.

When the party later returned to [[PC - The Cave]], Flint concentrated upon the strange connection he felt towards the creature and determined that it had travelled north towards [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]].

Its intended destination, the meaning of **Home**, and its exact relationship to Flint remain unknown.

The party is currently pursuing it north.
