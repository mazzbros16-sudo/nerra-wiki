The growl echoed across Needleberry.

As the party looked towards the centre of town, more of the creatures began to emerge from the streets and alleyways. They did not hunt. They gathered. One by one they turned towards the same point somewhere within the storm, moving with an unsettling discipline that felt more like soldiers than monsters.

Then the blizzard returned with full force.

The wind howled through the streets, swallowing the town beneath sheets of snow. Through the storm, the party caught only a brief glimpse of two crimson eyes watching from a rooftop before both the figure and its followers disappeared into the white.

With the cold becoming just as deadly as the creatures themselves, Bromm Hartwell called the party into the safety of his smithy.

Before joining the others, Dean noticed movement outside The Pincushion.

He found Edda Voss buried beneath the snow, her life already slipping away. Despite everything that had happened, her final thoughts remained with the town, speaking only briefly before passing away in Dean's arms.

The party spent the night sheltering inside the smithy as the storm raged outside. They spoke long into the evening, taking stock of everything they had witnessed. Ash warned of the dangers surrounding the occult and spoke of Malach Vey, whilst the others shared what little they understood of the creatures. Questions continued to outnumber answers.

Morning brought no relief. The storm had buried much of Needleberry beneath deep snowdrifts. After digging their way from the smithy, the party found the town already at work clearing roads, repairing buildings and accounting for those who had survived.

Soon afterwards they were summoned to the Town Hall. There, Irilard Belcons addressed the surviving townsfolk. Seven people had been lost during the night, and the creatures would almost certainly return. The town would prepare itself for another attack.

When the party revealed they had successfully killed one of the creatures, Belcons entrusted them with a task no one else could undertake. Find where they came from.

The investigation began by speaking with Mary Whittaker, the oldest resident they could find, in the hope she had heard stories of similar events.
Then to The Pincushion, where the party gathered what supplies remained to use against the creatures. Their search also took them through the graveyard before they eventually turned their attention towards the abandoned Needleworks.

Inside, they discovered the body of another victim hidden within an upstairs cupboard, bearing the same puncture wounds seen elsewhere throughout the town. Careful investigation revealed marks where one of the creatures had leapt through an upstairs window before disappearing into the surrounding forest.

Following the trail proved difficult beneath the fresh snow. Vadim eventually lost the tracks, though, without realising it, Flint slowly drifted to the front of the group and continued leading them in the correct direction before eventually stopping, seemingly unaware that he had done so. Ash and Dean soon rediscovered the trail, eventually leading the party to the entrance of an abandoned cave hidden amongst the hills beyond Needleberry.

Deep within the cave they found the remnants of an old camp, untouched for decades.

And amongst it, the creatures.

They stood perfectly still, eyes closed, waiting in complete silence. As the party turned to sneak out, a voice echoed through the darkness.

"...Persistence... unexpected..."

"...Will... still... persists..."

"...Incomplete..."

"...Custodian..."

As the final word faded, every creature opened its eyes. The party fled. Realising they could never outrun them through the tunnels, Dean spotted a boarded-up passage leading into a smaller chamber. They forced their way through just as the creatures closed the distance behind them.

Vadim narrowly escaped being overwhelmed before the wooden structure above the passage was brought crashing down, sealing most of the creatures behind it.

Only two made it through. Using a pair of mirrors to direct shafts of sunlight around the chamber, the party discovered the creatures' greatest weakness. Blinded by the light, one fell quickly before the others could break through the collapsing barricade.

With the sounds of more creatures drawing ever closer, the party climbed the ladder towards the surface, emerging into the winter daylight as the creatures below remained trapped beneath the mountain, unable to follow into the sun.
