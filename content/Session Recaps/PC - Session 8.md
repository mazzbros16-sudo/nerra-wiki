The party continued north towards [[PC - Waymeet]], travelling through increasingly empty winter country.

After several days on the road, they encountered two brothers, Tobias and Edwin Marrick. The Marricks are travelling cartographers and self-described "story-finders" who collect old maps, wartime accounts, local legends and stories of places that have disappeared from modern maps.

The party agreed to escort them safely to Waymeet. In return, the Marricks promised to add several unusual locations around the [[PC - The Stoneward Spine]] to the party's map, each associated with rumours of something interesting or valuable.

The brothers also spoke about the growing danger of the northern roads. While dwarven rule over the Stoneward Spine was hardly remembered fondly, their control of the mountains had kept many dangerous creatures contained. Since the end of that rule, travellers have increasingly reported creatures wandering further from the Spine.

## Feeding Aleksander

With [[PC - Aleksander]] needing blood, Dean volunteered rather than risk another traveller becoming his meal.

Under Ash's supervision, Aleksander fed from Dean without seriously harming him, establishing that he can feed from a willing person without killing them.

It also gave Dean his  first proper look beneath Aleksander's wrappings: an older, deathly pale man with white hair and grey eyes.

The following morning, a hungover Dean awoke to discover Ash attempting to cook breakfast with the precision of an alchemist and none of the instincts of a cook.

Meanwhile, [[PC - Flint]] went looking for eggs and instead returned with a rather dangerous alchemical root.

## The Spider

Later that day, the party found a deliberately felled tree blocking the road. A discarded crossbow, scattered coins and traces of frozen blood suggested an ambush, although there were no bodies.

While investigating, Ash discovered the real threat when a giant trapdoor spider erupted from beneath the snow.

Flint was badly poisoned and briefly went down during the fight, but Dean kept him alive while Ash weakened the creature. Aleksander eventually finished it by driving his sword through its head.

The spider's burrow revealed that the apparent bandits had themselves become its victims. Among their remaining possessions, the party recovered coin, healing supplies, a silver dagger, jewellery and other equipment.

More unusually, they found a heavy iron token stamped:

**HOLT CONSOLIDATED** On one side

**37** on the other

The Marricks recognised Holt Consolidated as an organisation connected to [[PC - Ironwall]], although the significance of the token remains unknown.

With the spider dead and the road ahead cleared, the party continued towards [[PC - Waymeet]].

The Marricks still owe them the map locations
