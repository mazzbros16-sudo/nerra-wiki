Following Belcons' death, the party found themselves trapped within the dining hall as the red-eyed monstrosity hurled itself repeatedly against the dwarven steel barrier protecting them from the rest of the manor. The wall held, but only just. Each impact sent tremors through the room, and it became increasingly clear that it would not withstand the creature forever.

As the party began urgently discussing how they might survive what was coming next, Flint suddenly suffered a violent malfunction. His voice distorted, dropping into a deeper register before breaking apart into mechanical static. Moments later his entire body locked up, his core glowing an ominous red. When Ash examined him, he discovered Flint was not damaged in the conventional sense. His systems remained functional, but something inside his mind appeared to be forcing him into a catastrophic shutdown state.

With Flint completely unresponsive and the creatures steadily breaking through the barrier, the situation rapidly deteriorated. Fortunately, a familiar voice called out through the walls.

Louis had survived.

From elsewhere within the manor, Belcons' loyal butler directed the party to a number of defensive systems hidden throughout the dining hall. With little time remaining, the party positioned themselves and prepared what traps they could. Mustard gas could be released into the centre of the room. A spear trap remained hidden within one wall. A flame trap ran through the ceiling above.

Then the steel wall finally began to fail.

A tear appeared in the metal, revealing glimpses of the enormous red-eyed creature beyond. It continued ripping apart the barrier while the first of the smaller creatures squeezed through. Combat began once again.

As the battle unfolded, Flint found himself elsewhere.

He awoke within a strange stone structure. Walking through unfamiliar corridors, he eventually entered what appeared to be a laboratory. Strange samples, biological remains and unfamiliar devices filled the room. Beneath a sheet in one corner stood a large glass chamber.

When Flint removed the sheet, he found himself staring at his own reflection.

Then dozens of reflections.

Hundreds.

Everywhere he looked, he saw himself.

The world spiralled apart around him until everything vanished.

When his vision cleared, he found himself standing before a large iron door bearing a simple question:

**Who are you?**

Behind him stood three doors.

Passing through the first, Flint found himself once again aboard a familiar ship. He stood amongst familiar faces as a crewman called for the ship's Thaumaturge. He helped guide the vessel exactly as he had countless times before.

The second door led to a different chapter of his life. Alone, he travelled through the winter wilderness. For perhaps the first time in his existence, he moved without responsibility, obligation or destination, guided only by a vague feeling that he was travelling towards something he could no longer remember.

The final door led somewhere much older.

Flint found himself seated behind a desk within a laboratory office. A dwarven figure entered and calmly delivered a report.

**"Custodian, specimen 12 has become hostile again, please take care of it."**

Without hesitation, Flint rose and moved into a containment facility. Reinforced chambers lined the walls. Beyond thick glass stood pale, motionless creatures remarkably similar to those attacking Needleberry.

The memory faded before he could learn more.

Back in reality, the battle worsened.

The creatures steadily poured through the breach as the red-eyed monstrosity continued tearing the wall apart. The party unleashed every trap available to them. Mustard gas filled the air and Ash ignited it with alchemical fire. Flames spread throughout the chamber while the spear trap shredded creatures unfortunate enough to advance through its path. Many of the lesser creatures were destroyed almost instantly.

The red-eyed creature continued forward, flesh melting but no signs of stopping.

As Dean, Ash and Vadim struggled to hold the line, Flint continued working through fragmented memories. Slowly, he pieced together the identity his damaged mind had buried.

After much deliberation, Flint found his designation.

Custodian.

As that understanding settled into place, something within him unlocked.

Flint awoke.

His core and eyes a deep red. He uttered 3 words " Kadun, Barum, Vornak" and all the creatures froze, including the red-eyed one.

The party fled outside through the window in an instant, watching to see if the creatures would follow. The red-eyed creature began to twitch, before exploding forward at blinding speed, and catching up to the party.

Flint attempted the dwarven command phrases, but the creature ignored them. Whatever authority he possessed was apparently insufficient.

The enormous creature confronted the party in no time, and all members stopped to meet it.

The creature struck.

Despite his efforts, the sheer force of the attack proved overwhelming. Already weakened from earlier battles, Vadim was impaled and hurled violently aside, no signs of life. Dean was then struck down, barely stable.

A brilliant act of ingenuity from Ash saw him notice the creature's hesitation to attack Flint, as he grabbed Dean's body and bunkered behind Flint.

For the first time, the monstrosity hesitated.

Its glowing eyes fixed upon the automaton.

The room fell silent.

After several moments, the creature spoke.

**"Custodian authority recognised. Fault in mission parameters. Returning Home"**

Without another word, the monstrosity turned and departed, abandoning the battle entirely.

The party returned to the manor to find the remaining creatures standing still, as they were in the cave.

The victory came at a cost.

In the aftermath, the party dealt with the remaining creatures, ensuring none would rise again. Dean performed a symbolic sacrifice on behalf of the town's dead before helping dispose of the remains scattered throughout the manor. Ash repaired damage to the building, secured the area and confirmed the settlement's defences had re-engaged.

Vadim's body was cremated.

Belcons' remains were gathered.

And for the first time since the attack began, Needleberry finally grew quiet.

While searching Belcons Manor, Dean spent time exploring Belcons' library and uncovered details surrounding the century-long Smoke War, including the Liberation of Vellor and the infamous Night of Red Smoke at Karnholt.

Far more troubling, however, was what the party had learned about Flint.

The creatures knew him and recognised his authority. They called him **Custodian**.

And now this creature was heading home, wherever that was.
