Having escaped the cave through the grate above, the party found themselves back beneath the open sky. Dean immediately broke into a run for Needleberry, with Flint following close behind, while Vadim and Ash made their way back at a steadier pace.

Dean and Flint headed straight for Belcons Manor, where Dean was greeted by Louis, one of Belcons' servants, and given a moment to catch his breath. Once Vadim and Ash arrived, the party were brought inside to meet with Irilard Belcons and explain what they had discovered.

The meeting quickly became heated.

Belcons believed the behaviour of the creatures suggested something more deliberate than a simple attack. There had been enough of them to inflict far greater casualties on Needleberry, yet they had withdrawn. If the creatures were choosing their targets, gathering the entire town in one place alongside the only people who had successfully fought them could result in a massacre.

Vadim disagreed. He accused Belcons of hiding away in his manor while the people of Needleberry were left to defend themselves, eventually storming from the room and declaring that he would rather die protecting the town than survive behind Belcons' walls. Flint followed him.

Dean remained behind. His history with Belcons gave him reason to trust his judgement, although he would only agree to the plan if the children of Needleberry could be brought to the manor and placed somewhere safe. Ash spoke with Belcons separately and came to understand his reasoning. Belcons was making an assumption, but if that assumption was correct, bringing the party to the Town Hall could place everyone gathered there in danger.

The party eventually regrouped on the slope outside the manor and discussed their options. Whatever they chose, separating would leave them with the worst of both plans. Vadim eventually agreed to stay at the manor, on the condition that he would remain outside and ensure the creatures came for them rather than the town.

The party returned to the Town Hall.

There, Dean addressed the people of Needleberry. He made no promise that everyone would survive the night, but told them that the party intended to draw the creatures away and that the town's children would be taken somewhere safer before they did.

The children were brought back to Belcons Manor and taken into the safe room beneath the house. With them protected, the party prepared for the night ahead.

Vadim positioned himself outside the manor as bait. Ash prepared a line of fire across the approach, rigged so that it could be ignited from inside using a vial and rope. Belcons stood ready to close the manor's second layer of defence, a massive dwarven-reinforced door capable of sealing the inner house.

Then they waited.

Hours passed after nightfall before Vadim finally noticed two red eyes watching from the tree line. More shapes were moving through the darkness, slowly approaching the manor.

Vadim looked towards them.

"I see you."

Then he ran.

Vadim made it inside. Ash triggered the trap. Fire spread across the approach as the creatures charged through it, and two burning figures burst through the entrance just as Belcons activated the reinforced wall.

The manor was sealed.

With the creatures now outside, Belcons showed the party more of the defences he had built into his home. Traps were hidden throughout the manor, including floor mechanisms, gas dispensers and further defensive walls protecting the cellar and safe room.

He also explained the origin of the enormous dwarven wall now protecting them.

It had once stood at Aerra Ungor, a settlement known before its colonisation as Matiel. Belcons had led the siege of Aerra Ungor during the Smoke War, a siege that lasted a year and a half. When it finally fell, he took the wall as recompense and eventually incorporated it into the manor.

For a time, the defences held.

Then Belcons noticed something.

The generator had gone quiet.

The machinery powering the manor had stopped.

Belcons explained that the generator was located within the manor near the older section of the property. Without it, the safe room could not be properly opened and sealed. Whatever safety the manor offered would mean little if they could not get the generator running again.

With the children already waiting below and the creatures somewhere outside the defences, the party began planning how they were going to reach it.

And that is where we left off.
