With the manor sealed and the generator silent, the party were left with little choice but to leave the relative safety of the dining hall and make their way towards the generator room. After some deliberation, and a considerable amount of criticism directed towards Belcons for the generator's positioning, the party settled on a route. Belcons attempted to defend the decision, explaining that the generator itself posed a significant danger if damaged and had deliberately been kept away from the areas of the manor he actually inhabited. This did little to stop the criticism.

The party decided to follow the rear wall of the manor, knowing that the doors connecting the nearby rooms to the main hall should provide some protection if they remained closed. Vadim made little effort to conceal himself, walking normally through the manor, while the others moved quietly behind him. Despite this, they reached the generator room without attracting the creatures.

Something was immediately wrong.

The door stood open. Velan had specifically been instructed to close it behind him.

Inside, they found the generator, a large piece of dwarven machinery made up of heavy metal components, pipes, valves and pressure systems. More importantly, it wasn't damaged. Nothing had been torn apart or destroyed. Somehow, the generator had simply been shut down.

There was little time to consider what this meant. Belcons explained that restarting the generator quickly would require several of them working together, and the party moved into position. As Dean entered the room, however, he looked upwards and noticed three of the creatures clinging silently to the ceiling above them.

Combat immediately broke out.

Vadim was brought down quickly, with Flint following not long afterwards, but the party began using the generator room itself against the creatures. Dean proved particularly resourceful, releasing a large pile of coal onto two of them before dousing the third in fuel, while also managing to provide healing to his fallen allies. Amongst also healing numerous allies, Ash ignited the fuel, setting the creature alight.

The creatures began to fall. Ash killed one with alchemist's fire while it remained buried amongst the coal. Flint brought down another with his blade, empowered through his exploit vulnerability. The final creature attempted to turn on Dean, only for Vadim to bring it down before it could bring him down.

With the immediate threat dealt with, the party turned their attention back to the generator. Ash opened the fuel valves and perfectly timed the final release. Dean operated the main pressure pump, while Vadim physically held a pressure pipe closed as the rapid startup placed enormous stress on the system.

The generator finally roared back to life.

With power restored, there was no reason to remain. The party and Belcons sprinted through the manor and made it back to the dining hall, the defensive doors closing behind them. Belcons immediately left for the vault, telling the party he would ensure the children were safely sealed inside.

He didn't return.

Instead, his voice eventually reverberated through the walls.

Belcons called out to the creatures. He invoked **Kharad Vorn**, demanding negotiation and asking what they wanted. For some time, there was no answer. Then, for the first time, the creature with the red eyes spoke.

It recognised revenge.

It spoke of enemies previously detected within the camp.

And it identified their deaths as a priority.

Belcons then addressed the party privately. He admitted that he had suspected the creatures might follow them and that bringing them to the manor had allowed him to test that theory. Their behaviour had confirmed his suspicions. In his mind, Needleberry could not hope to defeat an organised force of creatures this powerful.

But if they could be reasoned with, perhaps the town could still be saved.

Belcons offered them an exchange: the lives of the party, who had attacked and killed their kind, in exchange for the rest of Needleberry.

The creature accepted.

But it demanded that the exchange be conducted face-to-face, in accordance with Kharad Vorn.

Belcons agreed.

A safety wall suddenly sealed the party away from the vault. They heard the vault open, footsteps beyond the wall, and then the vault sealing again. When the safety wall withdrew, they saw Belcons standing some distance away.

And, for the first time, they properly saw the creature with the red eyes.

It towered over the others, its body made from muscle and sinew, with unnaturally rounded shoulders, powerful arms and animal-like legs supporting a distinctly unnatural humanoid frame. Its hairless face was sharp and skeletal, almost resembling the shape of a goat's skull beneath tightly stretched skin. Behind it stood nearly a dozen of the lesser creatures in perfect unison.

A visibly shaken Belcons indicated the party.

**"Very well. The terms of the exchange. There they are."**

The creature extended one enormous clawed hand towards him.

Belcons hesitated, then reached forward to accept it.

The creature tore his arm from his body.

Before Belcons' scream could finish, it tore off his head.

It crushed what remained in its hand and immediately charged towards the party.

Then the wall slammed shut.

The creature crashed against the metal on the other side, scratching and tearing furiously at the barrier as the entire manor rattled around them.

In the brief moment before his death, Dean noticed one final thing.

Belcons' remaining hand had moved to his hip.

And just before the creature killed him, **he pressed something.**

The rumbling of the building as the creature attacked the wall forecast the end of the session.
