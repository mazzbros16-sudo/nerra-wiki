# Session 1 - The First Night

The roads of Southern Aedryn proved unforgiving. Flint wandered the snow-covered roads for  days, unknowingly passing the same abandoned cart three times before eventually crossing paths with Vadim. Recognising the worsening weather and the signs of an approaching blizzard, Vadim led Flint towards the nearby mining town of Needleberry.

The town itself carried on much as it always had. Dean made his usual trip through the market square, collecting supplies for the evening's stew and speaking with familiar faces throughout the township. Flint and Vadim arrived in town where the automaton found unexpected kindness when a local smith repaired part of his damaged frame.

Despite the cold, life continued as normal, and as evening settled over the town, everyone gradually found themselves making their way towards The Bent Needle. The tavern filled quickly. Travellers sought shelter from the worsening weather, miners finished another day's work, and regulars settled in for the evening. Mayor Hammond made a brief appearance, personally delivering a revised taxation notice to Dean before leaving almost as quickly as he had arrived. 
Not long afterwards, Ash arrived. The sight of a Blightwarden drew uneasy looks from many of the townsfolk, though one young child approached him with curiosity rather than fear.

There were some disagreements among the adventurers as they argued about use of different magic, which lead to an altercation with a miner. Before a riot broke out, the window burst open.

One of the miners was dragged into the night before anyone had the chance to react. Darkness consumed the tavern as the wind extinguished every candle, and panic followed almost immediately. A body soon fell from above, grey-skinned and marked by blackened veins, before the party caught their first glimpse of a slender black figure carrying a young girl into the night.

Without hesitation, the group gave chase.

Following the creature's tracks through the snow, they arrived at The Pincushion. Inside, they discovered Edda Voss'  husband had already fallen victim to the creature. Before they could fully take in the scene, the creature ambushed Edda and left out the window. The group followed, and the creature attacked them from behind

The battle was fierce but brief. Through quick thinking, determination and a devastating strike with alchemist's fire, the party brought the creature down.

For Ash, the battle revealed something else. A familiar presence lingered around the creature. The Rift.

As the creature collapsed, the town fell strangely quiet. For only a moment. Then a deep, unnatural growl echoed across Needleberry. It did not come from the creature lying at their feet. It came from somewhere within the town.
