After weeks of snow, isolation and questionable navigation, the party finally emerged from the backroads beyond Needlebury and found civilisation again.

Unfortunately, they also discovered that the Marrick brothers, professional cartographers, had taken a wrong turn leaving Carnhalt and accidentally added several weeks to the journey.

Ahead lay the **Iron Road**, an enormous dwarven-built highway stretching towards Ironwall. Heated stone kept the snow from settling, iron rails ran through its centre, and freight moved along it almost constantly.

Then came the train.

For most, it was simply an impressive relic of dwarven engineering rebuilt for a new age. For Ash, confronted with the machinery of the Smoke War made real, it was something else entirely. His first instinct was considerably closer to _prepare for battle_ than _admire the scenery_.

Further along the road, the party discovered an even darker reminder of the war: the stripped shell of a dwarven war machine left beside the highway as a memorial to those it had killed.

Ash eventually reached one reassuring conclusion.

**It could be destroyed.**

The journey continued until traffic thickened and the scattered buildings surrounding the highway became **Waymeet**, a chaotic trade town that seemed to have grown around the Iron Road without anyone ever actually deciding to build a town there.

It did not take long for the party to discover another problem.

Their cart was falling apart.

A trip down **Tinker’s Row** revealed extensive damage to the axle and a full day of repairs ahead. Ash enthusiastically took the opportunity to work through the night as an apprentice cartwright, while the others headed into town.

Dean found the **Wayfarer’s Rest**, acquired accommodation and somehow turned being unable to afford very much into working behind the bar for the evening. Flint explored the markets for equipment suitable for what might await them in the mountains and spoke with **Rogar**, an orc from the Shattercoast, about Ironwall, the old dwarven roads and the dangers beyond.

By morning, however, Waymeet had a new problem.

A valuable shipment had been stolen.

The bells rang, inspections began, and every wagon leaving town was to have its cargo and ownership papers checked.

Which was unfortunate.

Because after some frantic searching, the party discovered that their cart had no ownership papers.

Mostly because they had stolen it.

And people without convincing paperwork have an unfortunate tendency to be sent to Ironwall to meet the **Mountain Sentinels**.

Luckily, Ash received one piece of advice.

If you need papers you shouldn't have, speak to the orc at the Wayfarer’s Rest.
